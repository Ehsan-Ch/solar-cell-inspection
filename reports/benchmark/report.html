Evaluation, model selection, and limitations
Feature familyRegularization CValidation average precision
  • Only annotation endpoints 0 and 1 are used for supervised training and binary metrics. Intermediate annotations are an exploratory challenge set, not relabeled ground truth.
  • Exact decoded-pixel duplicates are removed before splitting. Related cells from a common module may still occur in different splits. Bootstrap intervals resample images and do not account for this clustering.
  • Logistic outputs are screening scores and have not been independently calibrated as probabilities. The validation recall target is not a guarantee on new images.
  • Training-only feature scaling; model selection and threshold selection use validation only. No refitting or augmentation after selection. Ordinary photographs and field deployment are outside the benchmark.
